# Chabot local tools — recommendations

**Date:** 2026-09-06 · **Status:** adopted — owner greenlit drafting the
Navigator; remaining ideas awaiting owner picks · **Supersedes:** — ·
**Superseded-by:** —

Hyper-local tools for families of kids with ADHD and autism at Anthony Chabot
Elementary (OUSD, Rockridge). The bar for every idea: shippable in days by one
parent, maintainable by a PTA volunteer, immediately useful. Collaborative-first
tone throughout (owner rule, Aug 2026).

## The core five (proposed Sep 6, owner greenlit #1 to draft)

| # | Idea | Effort | What it is |
|---|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | **Chabot Family Navigator 1-pager** | Days | Print/PDF front-and-back: who to ask at Chabot for an SST / IEP eval / 504, the legal clocks (15-day assessment plan, 60-day evaluation, 30-day meeting), copy-paste ask scripts, OUSD/CAC/RCEB/DREDF map, community pointers. English + Spanish. **→ drafted: `navigator-draft.md`** |
| 2 | **"Chabot Neurodiverse Families" Google Group** | An hour | Private, parent-run list/forum. The school-granular knowledge exchange BPN threads prove people want: which camps work, which OTs have openings, how IEP meetings really go. Seed with 3–5 known families; the 1-pager advertises it. |
| 3 | **One-page dedicated webpage** | A weekend | Static page hosting the living Navigator + dates (CAC, IEP season), links, Group signup. Becomes the clonable "one page per school" template — the hyper-local Waypoint pattern. |
| 4 | **"Meet the Teacher" child profile 1-pager** | Days | Fill-in template a parent hands the teacher in week one: strengths, triggers, what regulation looks like, accommodations that work. Opens the relationship as partners — the ethos in paper form. |
| 5 | **IEP/504 season calendar + reminders** | A week | Shared calendar / monthly Group email mapping the school year to action windows: evaluations before winter, annual-IEP prep, 5th→middle-school transition in fall, ESY asks in spring. Deadlines are where families silently lose rights. |

## Additional ideas (round two, Sep 6)

Same bar. Roughly ranked by value-for-effort.

| # | Idea | Effort | What it is |
|---|------|--------|------------|
| 6 | **Substitute & yard-staff card** | Days | The child profile (#4) condensed to an index card that lives in the sub folder and with yard staff: "what dysregulation looks like for [child], what helps, who to call." The adults who know the kids least handle the hardest moments; this is the highest-leverage piece of paper in the building. |
| 7 | **Parent buddy match** | A week | Pair each new ADHD/autism family with a veteran Chabot IEP parent — one intro email, one coffee, an open door before the first IEP meeting. The Group (#2) makes matching trivial. DREDF-style peer support, at school scale. |
| 8 | **Vetted after-school & camp list** | Grows itself | A shared sheet, crowd-sourced from the Group: which after-school programs, camps, and classes actually handle ADHD/autism well — with parent ratings and "call this person" notes. The single most-asked question on every parent thread. |
| 9 | **Inclusion pledge + party tips ½-pager** | Days | A gentle classroom norm, PTA-distributed: whole-class invites or discreet ones; a five-line "hosting a kid with sensory needs" tip sheet (quiet corner, food heads-up, leaving early is fine). Costs nothing; changes birthdays. |
| 10 | **IEP binder starter kit** | Days | A folder-structure handout + records-request template: how to organize assessments, IEPs, work samples, and emails so the paper trail exists before it's needed. Pairs with the Navigator's "put it in writing" rule. |
| 11 | **Sensory map of Chabot** | A week, needs school buy-in | One-page map of regulation-friendly spots (quiet corners, library hours, where to go when the yard is too much) + calmest drop-off/pickup routes. Made *with* the school — good SST conversation starter. |
| 12 | **Back-to-school-night 5-minute pitch** | An evening | A short spoken pitch + flyer for room parents/PTA night: "1 in 9 kids here has an IEP or 504; here's the Group, here's the Navigator, here's the pledge." The distribution engine for everything above. |
| 13 | **Respite swap co-op** | Later | ND-family babysitting exchange — parents who get it, trading care. Highest trust bar; only works once the Group (#2) has real membership. Park until then. |
