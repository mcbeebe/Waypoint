# Chabot Google Group — setup kit

**Date:** 2026-09-06 · **Status:** draft v0.1 — ready to use the hour the
group is created; owner fills `[bracketed]` items · **Supersedes:** — ·
**Superseded-by:** —

*Everything needed to launch the "Chabot Neurodiverse Families" Google Group:
a 15-minute setup checklist, the five community norms, the welcome message,
the pinned first post, the invite email for seed families, and a moderation
playbook. All copy follows the partnership tone rule. After creation, paste
the group link into the Navigator's `[group link]` blanks and regenerate the
flyer QR page.*

---

## 1 · Create the group (15 minutes, groups.google.com)

1. Go to **groups.google.com** → **Create group**.
2. **Name:** `Chabot Neurodiverse Families`
3. **Group email:** `chabot-nd-families@googlegroups.com` (short enough to
   say out loud at pickup; if taken, try `chabot-neurodiverse-families`).
4. **Description:** paste from section 2 below.
5. **Welcome message:** paste from section 3 below.
6. Then open **Group settings** and set:

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Who can see group | Anyone on the web | So the flyer QR / Navigator link resolves to a "ask to join" page |
| Who can join group | Anyone on the web can ask | You approve each request — this is the front door |
| Allow external members | **On** | Parents without Gmail can join by email |
| Who can view conversations | Group members | The privacy promise — nothing is public |
| Who can post | Group members | — |
| Who can view members | Group members | Don't expose the member list |
| Conversation history | **On** | This IS the archive — the whole point |
| Message moderation | No moderation | Trust-first for members |
| New member restrictions | New member posts are moderated | Catches spam accounts; approve their first post, then they're free |
| Spam message handling | Moderate and notify | — |
| Subject prefix | `[ChabotND]` | Threads are findable in a crowded inbox |
| Email footer | On, include the group's norms link | Every email carries the exit and the rules |
| Who can manage members | Group owners and managers | — |

7. **Add a co-owner immediately** (Member moderation → promote to Manager or
   Owner): `[co-moderator name/email]`. Two moderators, never one.
8. Post the pinned first post (section 4), then send the seed invites
   (section 5).
9. **Update the Navigator and flyer:** paste the join link
   (`groups.google.com/g/chabot-nd-families`) into every yellow
   `[group link]` blank, and regenerate the flyer QR if you want it to point
   at the group instead of the Navigator page.

> A private, parent-run group for Chabot Elementary families raising kids
> with ADHD, autism, and other brains that work differently. We trade the
> real answers: which camps actually work, which therapists have openings,

>
> To join, tell us your connection to Chabot (kid's grade or teacher is
> plenty). Membership is Chabot families and staff-recommended friends only,

## 4 · The pinned first post ("Start here")

**Subject:** `[ChabotND] Start here — what this group is, and our five norms`

> Hi, I'm `[your first name]` — Chabot parent, and the one who hit "create
> group." Here's what this is and how we keep it good.
>
> **What this group is for:** the school-specific knowledge that exists
> nowhere else. Which after-school programs genuinely handle our kids.
> Which OTs have openings. What an SST at Chabot is actually like. You ask,
> someone who's been there answers, and the answer stays searchable for the
> next family.
>
> **Our five norms:**
>
> 1. **What's shared here stays here.** Screenshots and forwards break the
>    group. Don't.
> 2. **We're partners with our school.** The teachers and specialists want
>    our kids to succeed. Vent feelings, not names — praise by name, but
>    keep staff names out of the hard stuff. If something needs raising,
>    the Navigator has the friendly words to raise it well.
> 3. **Every question is a good question.** First-week-of-diagnosis
>    questions especially. Nobody here was born knowing what an IEP is.
> 4. **Advice is experience, not prescription.** We share what worked for
>    our kid. Doctors, therapists, and DREDF get the final word on medical
>    and legal questions.
> 5. **Our kids' privacy leads.** Share what you'd be comfortable with your
>    child reading someday. Initials are always fine.
>
> (And one small one: no sales pitches — vendor recommendations are gold,
> vendor self-promotion isn't. Check with a moderator first.)
>
> **Moderators:** `[you]` and `[co-moderator]`. Reply here or email us
> directly any time.
>
> Now — introduce yourself below if you like: kid's grade, one thing that's
> working, one thing that's hard. I'll go first in the comments.

## 6 · First two weeks (how a group comes alive)

- **Day 1:** pinned post up, your own intro in its comments, seed invites out.
- **Day 2–3:** post two real questions yourself — ones you genuinely want
  answered ("Anyone's kid done the `[local camp]` aftercare? How'd it go?").
  Nothing invites a first post like an answerable question.
- **Week 1:** ask each seed family, personally, to post one question or one
  recommendation. Ten posts in the archive changes how the room feels.
- **Week 2:** flyer goes out (backpack folder / PTA newsletter) with the QR.
  From here it grows itself.
- **Monthly, 10 minutes:** approve pending members, welcome new folks by
  name in a short post, re-pin anything that answered a big question.

## 7 · Moderation playbook (the three situations that will actually happen)

- **A join request you don't recognize.** Reply asking their Chabot
  connection ("whose class is your kiddo in?"). Room parents and the front
  office can confirm edge cases. When in doubt, a quick friendly email
  beats a silent decline.
- **A thread heats up about a staff member.** Don't delete — redirect,
  fast and kindly, in-thread: *"Totally hear the frustration — let's keep
  names out per our norms. `[Name]`, the Navigator has the friendly script
  for raising exactly this, and I'm happy to help you draft the ask —
  DM me."* The norm does the work; you just point at it.
- **A vendor or salesy post.** Approve-or-decline is yours (new members are
  moderated anyway). Standard reply: recommendations from parents are
  always welcome; self-promotion goes through a moderator, and the answer
  is usually a friendly no.
