<AnswerFirst>
  <b>No.</b> IHSS requires <b>Medi-Cal, not SSI</b> — there is no SSI requirement anywhere in
  the IHSS rules <Cite>MPP §30-757</Cite>. A child with a developmental disability who lives at
  home can qualify for Medi-Cal through <b>institutional deeming</b>, which ignores parental
  income entirely. Many families are wrongly told otherwise and give up before they apply.
</AnswerFirst>

The myth has an understandable origin: in California, an SSI award brings automatic Medi-Cal
with it, so for many families SSI came first and Medi-Cal followed. Somewhere between the
county office and the parent forums, that common sequence hardened into a fake requirement —
and families denied SSI over parental income assumed IHSS was closed to them too. It is not.

What to do instead: get Medi-Cal in place via institutional deeming (your Regional Center
service coordinator can help start it), then apply to your county IHSS office and request
protective supervision. Our step-by-step guide walks the whole path:
<a href="/guides/benefits/ihss-protective-supervision/">IHSS Protective Supervision: how
California families qualify</a>. And if you want to know whether SSI is worth pursuing anyway,
the <a href="/tools/ssi-deeming-calculator/">SSI deeming calculator</a> estimates how parental
income deeming affects your child's eligibility.
