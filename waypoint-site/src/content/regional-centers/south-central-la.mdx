<h2 class="qa-h" id="intake">How does intake work?</h2>

The intake process runs the same at every regional center [TBC: add this center's specific
intake channels and contacts once verified]. A formal diagnosis helps but is **not required
to start** — eligibility turns on "substantial disability," meaning significant functional
limits in **3 or more of 7 life areas** <Cite>WIC §4512</Cite>.

1. **Contact intake and say your child's age.** Under 3 routes to Early Start and the faster
   45-day clock; 3 and over goes through standard Lanterman intake.
2. **Gather what you have** — pediatrician notes, evaluations, IEP or preschool records.
   Missing pieces should not delay your request.
3. **Do the intake interview.** Describe a hard day, not your child's best day.
4. **Note the deadline.** The assessment is due within 120 days of intake <Cite>WIC §4643</Cite> —
   under 3, Early Start completes everything within 45 days of the referral
   <Cite>17 CCR §52086</Cite>. Calendar it the day you first call.
5. **Get the decision in writing.** If eligible, planning moves to your child's IPP
   <Cite>WIC §4646</Cite>; if denied, you must receive a written **Notice of Action** with
   your appeal rights.

<h2 class="qa-h" id="la-area">Which part of Los Angeles County does this center serve?</h2>

<AnswerFirst>
  Seven regional centers divide Los Angeles County between them, so "I live in LA County" is
  not enough to tell you which one is yours — <b>your address decides it</b>, and the
  boundaries follow LA County health districts, not city lines.
</AnswerFirst>

Part of Los Angeles County only — one of seven regional centers dividing LA County, where
catchment boundaries follow DDS health districts rather than county lines. SCLARC covers the
Compton, San Antonio, South, Southeast and Southwest health districts. Geographically its
area is bounded by Washington Blvd. on the north, La Cienega Blvd. on the west, and the
eastern and southern boundaries of Downey, Paramount, Compton, Dominguez Hills and Carson.
Cities served include Bell, Bell Gardens, Maywood, Paramount, Cudahy, Downey, Huntington
Park, South Gate, Compton, Lynwood, Gardena, Vernon and Carson, plus the South Los Angeles
area including the communities of Watts, Crenshaw, Hyde Park, Leimert Park, View Park,
Baldwin Hills, Florence/Firestone and West Adams.

### Who this center does not serve

Does not cover the rest of Los Angeles County, which belongs to the other six LA-area
regional centers. Ruling-out guide by neighbour: north of Washington Blvd. and
central/downtown LA, Hollywood, Glendale, Pasadena → Frank D. Lanterman Regional Center
(Central, Glendale, Hollywood-Wilshire, Pasadena health districts). West of La Cienega Blvd.
— Inglewood, Santa Monica and the Westside → Westside Regional Center (Inglewood, Santa
Monica-West health districts). South and southeast beyond Downey/Paramount/Compton/Carson —
Long Beach, Torrance, Bellflower and the Harbor area → Harbor Regional Center (Bellflower,
Harbor, Long Beach, Torrance health districts). East — East Los Angeles, Alhambra, Whittier,
Northeast LA → Eastern Los Angeles Regional Center. Farther east — El Monte, Pomona, the
Foothill area → San Gabriel/Pomona Regional Center. The northern portion of the county (San
Fernando/Santa Clarita/Antelope Valley) → North Los Angeles County Regional Center. It also
serves no county other than Los Angeles.
