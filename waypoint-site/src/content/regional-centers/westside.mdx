<h2 class="qa-h" id="intake">How does intake work?</h2>

The intake process runs the same at every regional center [TBC: add this center's specific
intake channels and contacts once verified]. A formal diagnosis helps but is **not required
to start** — eligibility turns on "substantial disability," meaning significant functional
limits in **3 or more of 7 life areas** <Cite>WIC §4512</Cite>.

1. **Contact intake and say your child's age.** Under 3 routes to Early Start and the faster
   45-day clock; 3 and over goes through standard Lanterman intake.
2. **Gather what you have** — pediatrician notes, evaluations, IEP or preschool records.
   Missing pieces should not delay your request.
3. **Do the intake interview.** Describe a hard day, not your child's best day.
4. **Note the deadline.** The assessment is due within 120 days of intake <Cite>WIC §4643</Cite> —
   under 3, Early Start completes everything within 45 days of the referral
   <Cite>17 CCR §52086</Cite>. Calendar it the day you first call.
5. **Get the decision in writing.** If eligible, planning moves to your child's IPP
   <Cite>WIC §4646</Cite>; if denied, you must receive a written **Notice of Action** with
   your appeal rights.

<h2 class="qa-h" id="la-area">Which part of Los Angeles County does this center serve?</h2>

<AnswerFirst>
  Seven regional centers divide Los Angeles County between them, so "I live in LA County" is
  not enough to tell you which one is yours — <b>your address decides it</b>, and the
  boundaries follow LA County health districts, not city lines.
</AnswerFirst>

Westside Regional Center serves only a portion of Los Angeles County — specifically the
Inglewood and Santa Monica-West Health Districts. (In LA County, regional center catchment
boundaries follow DDS health districts, not county lines; seven regional centers divide the
county.) Communities named in WRC's own catchment listing include: Bel Air, Beverly Hills,
Beverlywood, Calabasas, Century City, Culver City, El Segundo, Gardena, Hawthorne,
Inglewood, Lawndale, Lennox, Los Angeles (westside portions), Malibu, Marina del Rey, Mar
Vista, Pacific Palisades, Playa del Rey, Santa Monica, Topanga Canyon, Venice, Westchester,
West Los Angeles, and Westwood.

### Who this center does not serve

WRC does not cover the rest of Los Angeles County. Per the DDS health-district split
evidenced in search results: the Bellflower, Harbor, Long Beach and Torrance health
districts are served by Harbor Regional Center (so South Bay/Long Beach/Torrance-area
families are not WRC), and the Central, Glendale, Hollywood-Wilshire and Pasadena health
districts are served by Frank D. Lanterman Regional Center (so
downtown/Hollywood/Glendale/Pasadena families are not WRC). WRC also serves no county other
than Los Angeles. Caution: exact boundaries in edge cities are set by ZIP code — e.g.
Gardena appears in WRC's own published catchment list even though it sits near Harbor RC
territory — so borderline addresses should be checked against the DDS "Regional Center
Lookup" or WRC's catchment map rather than by city name alone. (Assignments for other LA-
County regional centers beyond Harbor and Lanterman were not seen in a snippet and are not
asserted here.)
