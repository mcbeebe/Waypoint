<h2 class="qa-h" id="intake">How does intake work?</h2>

The intake process runs the same at every regional center [TBC: add this center's specific
intake channels and contacts once verified]. A formal diagnosis helps but is **not required
to start** — eligibility turns on "substantial disability," meaning significant functional
limits in **3 or more of 7 life areas** <Cite>WIC §4512</Cite>.

1. **Contact intake and say your child's age.** Under 3 routes to Early Start and the faster
   45-day clock; 3 and over goes through standard Lanterman intake.
2. **Gather what you have** — pediatrician notes, evaluations, IEP or preschool records.
   Missing pieces should not delay your request.
3. **Do the intake interview.** Describe a hard day, not your child's best day.
4. **Note the deadline.** The assessment is due within 120 days of intake <Cite>WIC §4643</Cite> —
   under 3, Early Start completes everything within 45 days of the referral
   <Cite>17 CCR §52086</Cite>. Calendar it the day you first call.
5. **Get the decision in writing.** If eligible, planning moves to your child's IPP
   <Cite>WIC §4646</Cite>; if denied, you must receive a written **Notice of Action** with
   your appeal rights.

<h2 class="qa-h" id="la-area">Which part of Los Angeles County does this center serve?</h2>

<AnswerFirst>
  Seven regional centers divide Los Angeles County between them, so "I live in LA County" is
  not enough to tell you which one is yours — <b>your address decides it</b>, and the
  boundaries follow LA County health districts and ZIP codes.
</AnswerFirst>

Central Los Angeles and the Hollywood/Wilshire corridor, plus the Glendale, Burbank, La
Cañada Flintridge, La Crescenta, Eagle Rock and Pasadena area — the four LA County health
districts of Central, Glendale, Hollywood-Wilshire, and Pasadena. It is one of the smallest
LA-area catchments geographically: it does NOT cover the San Fernando or Antelope Valleys
(North Los Angeles County RC), the Westside or South Bay (Westside RC / Harbor RC), South LA
(South Central LA RC), East LA (Eastern LA RC), or the San Gabriel Valley beyond Pasadena/La
Cañada (San Gabriel/Pomona RC). Boundaries are set by ZIP code, so families near the edges
should confirm with the DDS lookup at dds.ca.gov/rc.

If you live near one of these boundaries, confirm your center with the state's own lookup
before you call — being sent back to start by the wrong center costs weeks
