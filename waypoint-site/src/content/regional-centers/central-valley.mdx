<AnswerFirst>
  Central Valley Regional Center serves families in <b>Fresno, Kings, Madera, Mariposa, Merced, and Tulare counties</b> — one of California's
  <b> 21 regional centers</b> providing services under the Lanterman Act
  <Cite>WIC §4500 et seq.</Cite>, regardless of your income. Once intake begins, the center
  owes you any needed assessment within <b>120 days</b> of intake <Cite>WIC §4643</Cite> —
  or, under 3, a completed Early Start evaluation and initial family service plan within
  <b>45 days</b> of the referral <Cite>17 CCR §52086</Cite>. Write down the date of your
  first contact. The clock starts then.
</AnswerFirst>

<h2 class="qa-h" id="intake">How does intake work?</h2>

The intake process runs the same at every regional center [TBC: add this center's specific
intake channels and contacts once verified]. A formal diagnosis helps but is **not required
to start** — eligibility turns on "substantial disability," meaning significant functional
limits in **3 or more of 7 life areas** <Cite>WIC §4512</Cite>.

1. **Contact intake and say your child's age.** Under 3 routes to Early Start and the faster
   45-day clock; 3 and over goes through standard Lanterman intake.
2. **Gather what you have** — pediatrician notes, evaluations, IEP or preschool records.
   Missing pieces should not delay your request.
3. **Do the intake interview.** Describe a hard day, not your child's best day.
4. **Note the deadline.** The assessment is due within 120 days of intake <Cite>WIC §4643</Cite> —
   under 3, Early Start completes everything within 45 days of the referral
   <Cite>17 CCR §52086</Cite>. Calendar it the day you first call.
5. **Get the decision in writing.** If eligible, planning moves to your child's IPP
   <Cite>WIC §4646</Cite>; if denied, you must receive a written **Notice of Action** with
   your appeal rights.
