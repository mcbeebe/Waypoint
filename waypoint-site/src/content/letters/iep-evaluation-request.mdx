<AnswerFirst>
  Once your district <b>receives a written request</b> for a special education evaluation, it
  has <b>15 calendar days</b> to respond with an assessment plan — and <b>60 days after your
  signed consent</b> to complete the evaluation <Cite>CA Ed Code §56321</Cite>. Email the
  letter below to the principal <b>and</b> the district's special-education director, and keep
  a dated copy: the clock starts on receipt, not on a phone call.
</AnswerFirst>

<DeadlineCallout>
  Assessment plan due within <b>15 calendar days</b> of your written request (school breaks
  over 5 school days pause the clock) · evaluation and IEP meeting within <b>60 days</b> of
  your signed consent <span class="cite">Ed Code §56321 · §56344</span>
</DeadlineCallout>

<h2 class="qa-h">After you send it</h2>

1. **Calendar day 15.** If no assessment plan has arrived by then, follow up in writing and
   reference your original letter's date — the district's response deadline is 15 calendar
   days, not business days <Cite>CA Ed Code §56321</Cite>.
2. **Sign the assessment plan promptly when it arrives.** The 60-day evaluation clock starts
   at your signed consent, not at your request — the sooner you sign, the sooner it runs.
3. **Check the plan's scope.** The evaluation must cover **all areas of suspected
   disability** — if a concern you raised isn't listed, ask in writing for it to be added
   before you sign.
