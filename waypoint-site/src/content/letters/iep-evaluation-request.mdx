<AnswerFirst>
  Once your district <b>receives a written request</b> for a special education evaluation, it
  has <b>15 calendar days</b> to respond with an assessment plan <Cite>CA Ed Code §56321</Cite> —
  and <b>60 days after your signed consent</b> to complete the evaluation and hold the IEP
  meeting <Cite>CA Ed Code §56344</Cite>. Email the
  letter below to the principal <b>and</b> the district's special-education director, and keep
  a dated copy: the clock starts on receipt, not on a phone call.

<h2 class="qa-h">After you send it</h2>

1. **Calendar day 15.** If no assessment plan has arrived by then, follow up in writing and
   reference your original letter's date — the district's response deadline is 15 calendar
   days, not business days <Cite>CA Ed Code §56321</Cite>.
2. **Sign the assessment plan promptly when it arrives.** The 60-day evaluation clock starts
   at your signed consent, not at your request — the sooner you sign, the sooner it runs.
3. **Check the plan's scope.** The evaluation must cover **all areas of suspected
   disability** — if a concern you raised isn't listed, ask in writing for it to be added
   before you sign.

<h2 class="qa-h">While the school clock runs: ask the Regional Center to evaluate too</h2>

The school evaluation is one of two free evaluation doors. If your child may have a
developmental disability — or is under 3 — start the **Regional Center's** evaluation as
well. The two clocks run in parallel, neither waits for the other, and the Regional Center
door is where respite and Medi-Cal help live.

1. **Call your Regional Center's intake line** and say: *"I'd like to request an intake and
   assessment for my child."* Those words are the formal trigger — anyone believed to have a
   developmental disability is eligible for intake, no doctor's referral or completed
   diagnosis needed to start <Cite>WIC §4642</Cite>.
2. **Write down the date of your call, and ask them to confirm it in writing.** For age 3 and
   up, intake starts within 15 working days and any needed assessment is due within
   **120 days** of intake <Cite>WIC §4643</Cite>. Under 3, Early Start must complete the
   evaluation and initial family service plan within **45 days** of the referral
   <Cite>17 CCR §52086</Cite>.
3. **Not sure which of the 21 centers is yours?** The
   <a href="/tools/regional-center-finder/">Regional Center finder</a> answers in seconds —
   and the <GatedLink href="/guides/regional-centers/">Regional Center guide</GatedLink> has the whole
   playbook, from intake through the IPP meeting.
