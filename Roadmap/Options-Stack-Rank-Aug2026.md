## Rejected (evidence-closed)

- **Consumer subscription as the primary engine** — its own math (needs ~126k–250k registered CA users) and Undivided's 7-year retreat prove it. Survives only as option #2's modest tier.
- **Core IEP compliance software** — SEIS is a ~$10/student government near-monopoly across 136 SELPAs, with a free state template landing June 2026. Unwinnable.
- **Code 108 as specified** — serves consumers who are themselves parents; wrong population (audit finding, contradicted by DDS service definitions).
- **Per-family app licenses to RCs** — no service code for software; the system buys human hours.
- **Direct employer product** — consolidating category, consultant-gated 12–18 mo cycles, RethinkCare incumbent.
- **Advocate take-rate marketplace** — no take-rate model has ever survived in this category (listing fees only).
- **Facilitator + provider to the same family** — statutorily barred (W&I §4685.8). Separate books, enforced in-product.
