Waypoint Gap Report

What's meaningfully missing from the Supabase app versus the GAS MVP and the v2.0 PRD. The headline: most "missing" features are already built — they're just unreachable. The app ships a flat five-tab bar with no stack navigator, so 18 of 23 screens, three of five AI backend actions, and 20 of 32 database tables have no path a user can follow to them.

1 · The root cause: no stack navigator

One structural gap explains most of the others.

The entire navigation surface is App.tsx (Welcome → Onboarding → Main) plus a five-tab bar in MainTabs.tsx: Home, Ask AI, Tracker, Calendar, Profile. There are no Stack.Screen registrations beyond that, no modal routes, and no headers (so no header buttons either). Every navigation.navigate() call in the app targets a sibling tab. Nothing can open a detail screen, and no tab links to any of the 18 orphaned screens sitting in src/screens/main/.

Directly affects the starter plan we just shipped

The seeded onboarding actions carry call scripts, step-by-step checklists, document lists, and draft letters — but the Tracker has no detail view. Tapping a card just cycles its status (ActionsScreen.tsx:215). A finished 448-line ActionDetailScreen exists to display all of that content and is orphaned. Until it's registered on a stack, the richest content in the app is invisible.

4 · In the GAS MVP, with no counterpart at all

These have to be ported, not wired. Ordered roughly by product impact.

FeatureStatusWhat the GAS MVP does
Journey Map Absent The emotional centerpiece: 9 diagnosis-specific journeys (autism, Down syndrome, CP, ADHD, ID, epilepsy…) rendered as a phased timeline with agency/deadline chips per phase, milestones, alerts, and an animated "📍 You are here" badge matched to the child's age. It's the reveal moment after onboarding — the new app goes straight to tabs.
RC lookup + Agency Directory Absent A database of all 21 California Regional Centers with a ZIP-prefix lookup (plus boundary-ZIP overrides and a county fallback picker), feeding a directory of 8 agencies — each with phone, services, statutory rights, and a "watch out" pitfall, personalized to the family's actual RC. The new app collects ZIP at onboarding and then writes regional_center: null — the lookup data was never ported.
Letter & draft generator Absent 12 legally-grounded templates (assessment request, records request, PWN request, CDE complaint, DDS 4731 complaint, IHSS appeal, insurance appeal, IEE…) × 3 user-selectable tones (friendly ≤100 words / professional / strong with citations), an editable draft modal with placeholder highlighting, and a Gmail-compose handoff. The new app can email a chat response but has no template system. PRD F8 expects this plus a communication log.
RC Reimbursables guide Absent A dedicated screen listing 11 fundable service categories with POS billing codes, typical costs, and insider notes ("physician note required — most families don't know this exists"). One of the most differentiated pieces of content in the product.
Frustration flows & escalation engine Absent "How are things going?" check-ins branch into decision trees (RC slow → what → how long) that inject statute-aware escalation actions — detecting timeline violations against Lanterman deadlines (§4642/§4643/§4646) and chaining 4731 / CDE / IMR complaint actions with pre-written drafts. This is the product's adaptive advocacy layer.
Follow-up prompts Absent Completing key actions asks "How did it go?" (intake scheduled / voicemail / refused) and injects the right next action — including appeal and complaint paths. The new app's follow-ups are chat suggestion chips, a different thing.
Learn More + eligibility explainers Absent 15 in-app explainer modals (SSI, IHSS, IEP, IPP, Lanterman, Fair Hearing, SB 946…) and 3 "Do I qualify?" eligibility checkers linked from action cards. Some of this text survives inside our ported action descriptions, but the linkable explainer system doesn't exist.
Email analyzer Absent Paste an agency email thread → extract promises, timeline violations, red flags (budget excuses, soft denials), applicable law, and a ready-to-send response letter. Notably it was unreachable in the GAS UI too — but both a regex and a full AI implementation exist to port.
Chat feedback + graceful fallback Absent 👍/👎/⚠️ feedback buttons that back-fill an interaction log, and an AI-failure fallback that shows the user's top pending actions and their RC's tappable phone number instead of an error.
QA Testing Lab Absent 39 seeded prompt-regression tests with category/tone accuracy scoring and a metrics dashboard. Internal tooling, but it's how prompt quality was maintained across 12 sprints.
SMS "text myself" reminders Absent Every action's reminder line opens the native SMS composer pre-filled via an sms: deep link — a zero-infrastructure reminder channel. (We preserved the reminder text in follow_up_note, so the data is ready.)

5 · Cross-cutting quality gaps

Not features, but they decide whether the features above actually function.

GapDetail
NotificationsA complete 254-line useNotifications deadline-reminder hook has zero importers, and expo-notifications has no plugin config or permission strings. PRD F9 (Phase 1!) is unmet. The GAS email-reminder system was also never user-facing — reminders have never actually shipped in either codebase.
i18n coverageThe mechanism and a language switcher exist (en/es/vi), but only 3 files use translations — tab labels and a few Navigator/Profile strings. Home, Tracker, Calendar, and every orphaned screen are hardcoded English. (The GAS MVP had the same disease: Spanish covered ~22 landing-page strings, and its backend supported es/vi/tl/zh that the UI couldn't select.)
Offline synclib/offlineSync.ts is orphaned; useActions and useExpenses each hand-roll their own queue, and nothing replays queues on reconnect — NetworkBanner only displays a message.
Crash reporting@sentry/react-native sits in devDependencies while App.tsx imports it at runtime — a production build with a DSN set would fail to resolve it.
Config debtexpo-router is a dead dependency (React Navigation is used); EAS project ID and updates URL are "YOUR_EAS_PROJECT_ID" placeholders; ALL_REMAINING_MIGRATIONS.sql duplicates the migrations directory as a second source of schema truth; the version string on Profile says v2.0.0 while app.json says 1.0.0.
Accessibilitylib/accessibility.ts exists but only one helper is used once; screen-reader announcements, labels, and touch-target constants have no callers. (The GAS MVP's a11y was also thinner than documented.)

6 · Suggested sequence

Ordered by leverage — wiring before porting, porting before building new.

  1. 1

    Add a stack navigator and register the orphaned screens days, not weeks

    Wrap each tab in a stack; register ActionDetail, Documents, DocumentAnalysis, Resources, Blog, Expenses, and the legal pages; make Tracker cards open the detail view. This single change unlocks ~6,500 lines of finished work — including making the starter-plan scripts and checklists visible.

  2. 2

    Fix the ProfileScreen save stubs small

    Load and persist child name, RC status, and IEP status (the migration-012 columns exist now); regenerate the starter plan when they change, mirroring the GAS profile-save behavior.

  3. 3

    Configure Google OAuth config + console work

    Create the OAuth client IDs, add the env vars and the google-signin config plugin. This one setup unblocks both Gmail send and Calendar sync, which are otherwise finished.

  4. 4

    Port the California data layer mostly data transcription

    RC database + ZIP lookup (fixes regional_center: null at onboarding), the 8-agency directory, and the 11-category reimbursables guide. All three are structured data in the GAS files, ready to lift.

  5. 5

    Port the Journey Map medium

    JOURNEY_MAP_DATA transcribes directly; build one timeline screen and show it at onboarding completion. Consider the richer 5–6-phase versions from Waypoint-Journey-Maps.jsx, which the GAS port had coarsened.

  6. 6

    Finish the document pipeline medium

    Configure expo-document-picker, connect DocumentsScreen to the real upload hook, then route uploads into the OCR → IEP-analysis pipeline. This delivers both "file keeper" and "IEP Clarity reader" from parts that already exist.

  7. 7

    Then the adaptive layer larger

    Follow-up prompts, frustration flows with statute-aware escalations, the letter/template generator, chat history UI, notifications config, and analytics writes — the pieces that made the MVP feel like an advocate rather than a tracker.
