The Escalation Ladder
Regional Center · climb one rung at a time — each rung creates the paper trail the next rung needs. Every rung has a one-tap letter.
1
Put it in writing + request an IPP review
30 DAYS
State the gap, ask for the meeting. The RC must hold the review within 30 days of your written request — no webinar, orientation, or "process" gates this right.
✍️ Send the review request →
W&I Code §4646.5(b)
2
Demand the NOA — then appeal it
60 DAYS
Any denial, reduction, or termination must come as a written Notice of Action. No NOA = demand one. Appeal within 60 days; file within 30 days and existing services continue during the appeal ("aid paid pending").
✍️ Demand the NOA →
W&I Code §4710.5 — windows verified at build
3
§4731 complaint to the RC director
20 WORK DAYS
For rights violations (not just service disagreements): the director must respond with a written proposed resolution within 20 working days. Escalates to DDS if unresolved.
✍️ File the §4731 complaint →
W&I Code §4731 · template already in Letters
4
Bring in a free advocate
FREE
OCRA (Office of Clients' Rights Advocacy) has an attorney assigned to every Regional Center — free, by law. Disability Rights California takes systemic cases. You never have to climb alone, and you never have to pay.
📞 Find your OCRA advocate →
Why a ladder, not a list. Order matters: the written record from rung 1 is the evidence for rung 2; the NOA from rung 2 is what an advocate at rung 4 works with. Skipping rungs weakens every later one.
