Is it working? Check the data
YOU ARE HERE · NEW STAGE
The IEP isn't a hope — it's a contract with numbers. Check the numbers each grading period:
📊
Progress reports on every goal — due at least as often as report cards. Goals must have measurable baselines; "making progress" without a number is not a report.
🧾
Service delivery logs — minutes delivered vs minutes on the IEP. Shortfalls are owed back (compensatory education).
🚩
Slow or no progress is evidence for more services — never a reason to lower the goals. A district that can't produce data is the red flag itself.
✍️ Request progress data + service logs →
⏱ Records clock: districts must provide records within 5 business days of a written request (Ed Code §56504). New letter template drafts this in one tap.

If the district can't deliver
The options most families are never told exist:
NPA / NPS — the district funds a non-public agency or school when it can't provide FAPE itself.
Compensatory education — missed minutes are owed back. (Honest note: no fixed statute clock; it's a remedy in complaints and due process.)
ESY — extended school year when your child regresses over breaks and recoups slowly.
504-only lane — accommodations without specialized instruction, when that's the right fit.
