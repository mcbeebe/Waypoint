Living with services — check the delivery
YOU ARE HERE · NEW STAGE
The IPP promises hours. Vendors deliver them — or don't. Compare authorized hours vs delivered hours (ask the provider for service logs). A vendor waitlist does not pause the Regional Center's obligation: they must find another vendor or another way to deliver. Log every gap in your Paper Trail.
⏱ Honest clock: none is fixed for delivery gaps. The annual IPP review is the backstop — your 30-day requested review is the accelerator.

Not working? The escalation ladder
NEW STAGE
Four rungs, in order — each with its own clock and its own letter. It starts friendly and collaborative; the tone firms up only as rungs go unanswered. Most families are never told rungs 2–4 exist. See the full ladder →
1 · Friendly written ask + IPP review request 30 days
2 · Request the written NOA, appeal if refused 60 days
3 · §4731 complaint to the director 20 working days
4 · OCRA / Disability Rights California — free
