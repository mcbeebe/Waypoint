What the wording rests on

  • 45 calendar days from when the regional center receives the referral covers the evaluation, the assessments and the initial IFSP meeting. Sources: 34 CFR §303.310 and Gov. Code §95020(b). The app already registers §303.310 (verified 2026-08-29).
  • The pause applies only for documented exceptional family circumstances, or while consent is outstanding despite repeated documented attempts (§303.310(b)). DDS does not grant formal extensions.
  • Tone: the first step is a check-in ("ask"). Once past due, the next step is a follow-up that still asks. Home states the status of the answer and never names an actor who failed (CLAUDE.md tone rule).
