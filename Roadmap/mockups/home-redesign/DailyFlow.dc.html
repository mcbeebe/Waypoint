CONCEPT A · A WEEK IN THE LIFE
The daily flow — four ways a real day goes
The design target from the audit: the 45-second check-in is the norm, and closing the app guilt-free is a success state. Waypoint watches between opens (event-driven push, honest clocks) so the parent doesn't have to patrol.
1 · THE NORMAL MORNING — most days, ~45 seconds
Opens with coffee
Since-line: "Since Tuesday: nothing new. The district owes a reply by Sep 12."
→
Reads the One Thing
"DUE TODAY — OT session 3:00 PM" or one 20-second question, or…
→
…the calm state
"Nothing has a clock on it today. Your request is tracked — we'll tell you if Sep 12 passes. You can close the app."
→
Closes the app
Guilt-free. That's the product working.
2 · THE AGENCY REPLIED — the event-driven day
Push at 4:12 PM
"Lilia Talavera replied about the standing frame." (The push IS the patrol — no daily checking needed.)
→
Card: "Read Lilia's reply"
Read-first, always — opens the request's case file with the reply on the thread.
→
Drafts on the case
"Draft your response" — Waypoint proposes, parent edits; nothing sends until Send.
→
Done state
"Sent — on the case record. That was the most important thing today."
3 · THE HEAVY DAY / THE VETERAN SWEEP — triage by skipping, ~2 minutes
One Thing #1
"OVERDUE — IPP meeting, day 41 of 30." Does it, or taps Not today —
→
…card advances in place
Next-ranked item slides in. Skipping IS triage — no scrolling, and every skip is listed in Later with Undo.
→
Wants the full board?
One tap: Everything else → Waiting (every clock) and This week; deeper density lives in Actions & Calendar tabs, unchanged.
→
Before a meeting
Case file → 📄 Export — the dossier in hand, free.
4 · "I NEED SOMETHING NOW" — pull, not scroll
Types or talks
"diapers" · "they said no" · "log a call" · "school called" — the tell-Waypoint row is always right under the card.
→
Matched → direct chip
Tool chips appear as they type; one tap lands on the screen. Crisis words open the two-question intake first.
→
Unmatched → Ask AI
The exact words go to the Navigator with Home context. No dead ends, in en/es/vi.
→
Browsers still browse
Prefer poking around? The Tools doors are one tap down, unchanged from what you use today.
Weekly-or-rarer surfaces (Journey, Process Map, Money guides, Documents, Family Sharing) are reached through the doors or search — the same two taps they cost in hybrid v2 today. The only things that moved further away are the ones the audit showed were hurting: dead widgets, duplicate notices, and filler cards.
