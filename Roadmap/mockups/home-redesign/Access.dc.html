CONCEPT A · WHERE EVERYTHING LIVES
One card on top — nothing removed from reach
The One Thing card only changes what Home leads with. Every screen in the app keeps its place in the same five-tab structure, and every tool keeps the hybrid-v2 Tools area you just shipped — demoted exactly one tap, never deleted. Three access speeds, by how often a family actually needs each thing:
ZERO TAPS · ALWAYS ON SCREEN
The One Thing card — deep-links straight into today's job: a reply opens its case file, a deadline opens the letter, a calendar item opens the day.
The tell-Waypoint row — search + crisis entry + Ask AI in one field. Typing filters all 20 tools live ("diapers" → Money, "they said no" → Letters, "IEP" → IEP tools); anything unmatched goes to the Navigator with the exact words. The "nothing found" dead end no longer exists.
The tab bar — unchanged: Home · Ask AI · Actions · Calendar · Profile. Every stack behind those tabs is untouched.
ONE TAP · THE EVERYTHING-ELSE LAYER
One collapsed row with true counts ("2 this week · 1 waiting on the district · Tools") expands in place to:
• This week — dated lines → Calendar
• Waiting — open requests vs their legal clocks → case files
• Later — set aside by you — every deferral, with its return date and Undo
• Contacts — RC tap-to-call · write instead
• Tools — the hybrid-v2 area verbatim: Letters / Requests & Deadlines / Sent & Received with live badges, plus the four doors (Understand · Money · your child's records · More)
The layer remembers being open — a parent who likes browsing can keep it expanded and Home reads like today's Tools page with one smart card on top.
SEARCH-FIRST · TYPE OR TALK
For the other 15 tools a family touches monthly, hunting a grid was never the fast path — typing is. The composer carries the full search vocabulary from hybrid v2 plus the crisis words the audit found missing (suspended, restraint, police, moving, hospital).
"diapers" → Money door → RC Funding Guide
"school called" → crisis intake, two questions
"export my records" → Paper Trail share
"appeal" → Letters, appeal template
Voice input for parents who don't type English comfortably — same routing, es/vi included.
