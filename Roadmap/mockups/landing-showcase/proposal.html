Three calls before building

1

How free should IEP analysis be?

In the app today, IEP document analysis is a Premium feature (entitlements.ts). "Free" can mean three different things.

AFree goal check on the site; full analysis stays Premium RecommendedThe parent pastes one goal and gets an instant check of the five parts a measurable goal needs. Rule-based and runs in their browser. Nothing is uploaded and there's no AI cost. The full AI analysis is shown as the upgrade.
BOne free full AI analysis per free accountStronger hook for sign-up. Changes the free tier, so app entitlements and pricing copy change too.
CAnonymous AI analysis on the website Not recommendedNeeds a new Edge Function (not covered by CI), rate limiting and AI cost, and means handling a child's IEP with no account.
