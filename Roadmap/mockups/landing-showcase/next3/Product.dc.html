3 · LETTERS

The friendly email, already written

Requests and follow-ups, filled in with your child's name, the date and the citation. The first ask is always collaborative; you choose when to firm it up. Send from your own Gmail and replies come back next to the request, or open it in any mail app. Drafts in English, Spanish or Vietnamese.
