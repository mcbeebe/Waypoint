Can I ask for a behavior assessment at the IEP meeting?
Yes — and you don't have to wait for the meeting. Put it in writing now so the timeline starts.
YOUR RIGHTYou can request an IEP meeting at any time; the district must hold it within 30 days of your written request (Ed Code §56343.5).
GOOD TO KNOWAsking for the assessment in writing starts a separate 15-day clock for an assessment plan (Ed Code §56321).
WATCH OUTIf the team declines, ask for Prior Written Notice — it keeps your record and your options.
