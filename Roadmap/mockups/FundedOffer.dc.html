Who pays for this?
While you enroll: your Regional Center pays. Up to $1,000 for Leo's person-centered plan and up to 40 hours of transition help — billed to the Regional Center, not to you.
After you're enrolled: a line you approve in Leo's budget. You set the price with us, see it in the plan, and can stop at any time.
We never sell services on Leo's plan. State law (W&I §4685.8) makes your facilitator independent — our only loyalty is to you.
