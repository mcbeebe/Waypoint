How Waypoint Works
It isn’t a one-time checklist. Every family cycles through the same four moves — around and around, as things change.

Tell — tell Waypoint what’s going on: your child’s diagnosis, age, and where things stand with school or your Regional Center.
Plan — get one next right step, checked against California law (Lanterman Act, IDEA, Medi-Cal, SSI) — not a giant checklist.
Act — send a ready-to-go, friendly ask. Waypoint drafts it; you review and send it.
Track — Waypoint watches the clock. No answer in time, and the next ask gets a little firmer.
A reply comes in, a deadline passes, a new stage opens — and the loop starts again at Tell or Plan.
HOW BEST TO USE IT
Start at Home, not the menu — it always shows the one thing to do next.
Type your situation in plain English — no legal terms required.
Review before you send — you’re always in control of what goes out.
UNDER THE HOOD
Matches your situation to California disability law — Lanterman Act, IDEA, Medi-Cal, SSI — with the citation attached.
Every ask starts a legal clock (like a 60-day IPP timeline) — Waypoint tracks it for you.
Tone starts collaborative and only firms up — assertive, then adversarial — if an agency goes quiet.
