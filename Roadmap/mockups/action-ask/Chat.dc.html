I’ve finished “Apply for Medi-Cal” — I submitted the application. What happens next, how long should it take, and what should I be watching for?
Nice work getting it in. Here’s the shape of what comes next.

The county has 45 days to decide a Medi-Cal application (20 days if it’s on disability). You’ll usually get a letter asking for proof — income, residency, your child’s Social Security number — before anything else happens. That request is normal, not a problem, but it has its own clock: miss it and the application can be denied for paperwork rather than on the merits.
WATCH FOR
A “Request for Information” envelope from your county office. Photograph it and send it back the same week.
NEXT STEPS
1. Save your confirmation number somewhere you can find it.
2. Around day 30, call to check the file is complete.
3. When the card arrives, ask about EPSDT — it covers all medically necessary care under 21.
