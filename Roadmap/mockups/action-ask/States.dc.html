What the composer opens with
One button, no starter chips — the owner’s call, Oct 14. The status-awareness lives in the sentence, which is what the model reads; the question after it is the parent’s own. The caret shows where their typing begins.
Nothing is sent until they tap send. A prefill is text in a box — that is the one behaviour separating this from the older ask param, and it is pinned by a test.

3 · DONE — THE SCREENSHOT’S CASE

4 · DONE, AND AN ANSWER IS PAST DUE
About “Apply for Medi-Cal” — I’ve marked it done (Aug 20). An answer on it is past due.
The riskiest string in the feature. “An answer on it is past due” — not “they missed the deadline,” not “they owe you.” Same rule homeTriage follows, and a banned-phrase test now walks all 48 state×locale combinations looking for the other framing.
5 · THE DATE HAS GONE BY
About “Apply for Medi-Cal” for Ben — I haven’t started it yet; it has 8 steps. It was due Aug 24, and that date has passed.
A fact about the date, not a verdict on the parent — no “late,” no “overdue,” no “behind,” all three banned by test. And the child named here is the one this action is attached to, never the primary child: that exact fallback once put “Ana’s plan” on an email about Ben.
6 · ES / VI — TRANSLATED, NOT COPY-PASTED
Sobre “Apply for Medi-Cal”: lo marqué como hecho (Oct 2).
Về “Apply for Medi-Cal” — tôi đã đánh dấu hoàn thành (Oct 2).
The action’s title is a proper noun and survives translation untouched — asserted, because a “helpful” future translation of it would stop matching the action the parent is looking at.
