MOCKUP NOTE — THE FIVE RULES draftQuestions.ts IS BUILT TO KEEP
1. Never more than three. A question the profile already answers is not asked — it is asserted on the draft with a “change this” tap.
2. Chips, not prose. The freeform box is an escape hatch, never the path.
3. Every answer changes the letter. A question that doesn’t alter the text is decoration and gets deleted — and that is a test, not a comment.
4. Collaborative-first. Tone follows the stage, not the mood: first ask → friendly, past due → firmer, formal step → on the record. Override is one tap.
5. Trilingual (en/es/vi) with locale-parity tests, like every other string.
