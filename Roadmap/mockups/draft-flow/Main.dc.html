The draft flow
Waypoint already tells a parent what the next thing is. It does not yet hand them the words. This is the phase that closes the gap between “I know I should do something” and “I sent it.”
THE BRIEF
“Beat Undivided in user experience and prompting and empowering users; serving them up with not only the Next One Thing, but drafting that email, verbiage and guiding them. Undivided has lots of content but their website and app are overwhelming with info. Ours is focused, accessible and action oriented.”
TODAY — WHERE THE COURAGE LEAKS OUT
The One Thing card’s CTA drops the parent into the Letters screen: a picker of 22 templates, a picker of 3 tones, and a 96px empty box asking them to describe what they need.
That is a second decision, in a different vocabulary, at the exact moment their courage is highest. The card decided for them; then we asked them to decide again.
PROPOSED — THE CARD PRODUCES A DRAFT, NOT A FORM
Tap → two or three questions, answered by tapping chips → a finished letter with your child’s name, your dates and the statute already in it → send.
The parent never picks a template. They never pick a tone unless they want to. They never face an empty box.

Where we compete — and where we don’t
