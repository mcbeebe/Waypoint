Rejected — closed by evidence

OptionWhy it is rejected (verified evidence)
Consumer subscription as the primary engineNeeds ~126k–250k registered CA users for $1M; ~75% of the target market is low-income/Medi-Cal; Undivided's 7-year retreat to $19/mo + free human-led onboarding proves DTC-only fails here.
Core IEP compliance softwareSEIS is a government near-monopoly (~700k students at ~$9.50/student/yr) across 136 SELPA buyers; a free state-digitized IEP template lands June 2026.
Code 108 coaching as specifiedDDS Jan-2026 service definitions: code 108 serves consumers who are themselves parents (adults with I/DD) — not parents of child consumers. Wrong population.
Per-family app licenses to Regional CentersNo service code exists for software; the DDS payment architecture buys human service hours against POS authorizations.
Direct employer benefits productCategory consolidating (Joshin sold, Grayce wound down); RethinkCare incumbent; consultant-gated 12–18-month cycles.
Advocate take-rate marketplaceNo take-rate model has ever survived in this category — surviving models are $10/mo listing fees or SaaS.
Facilitator + provider to the same familyStatutorily barred: W&I §4685.8 defines an Independent Facilitator as someone not otherwise serving the participant. Separate books of families, enforced in-product.
