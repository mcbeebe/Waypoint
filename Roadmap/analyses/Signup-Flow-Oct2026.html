How a visitor creates an account

The path from a visitor arriving to a family landing on Home, traced from the code. A visitor signs in first, then completes a six-step setup. The family's records aren't saved until the last step.

Friction and risk

Things worth knowing

  • Quitting partway loses the setup answers. They're held in memory until step 6. The account itself still exists.
  • Web visitors can't use Apple sign-in. If the Google setting isn't configured, email is their only option.
  • Google asks for Calendar and Gmail on the phone apps. That's a lot to ask on the first screen, before any value is shown.
  • Confirm-email sends the parent away. If it's on, they have to leave the app, click the link, and come back to sign in.
Not checked in production

To confirm what visitors really see

  • Supabase Auth: whether "Confirm email" is on
  • Supabase Auth: whether the Apple and Google providers are turned on
  • Vercel: whether EXPO_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_WEB_CLIENT_ID is set for production
  • Supabase Vault and secrets: whether sign-up alerts are configured
