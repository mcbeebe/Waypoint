Subject: Service-code guidance — parent training & navigation supports for families of child consumers

Dear [Name],

I'm the founder of Waypoint, a California benefit corporation building navigation and family-support services for parents of children served by Regional Centers, based in RCEB's catchment. Before we submit a vendorization application, I'd be grateful for written guidance on six questions:

1. **Which service code(s) would RCEB use to purchase structured training, coaching, or navigation support delivered to the parents of a child consumer** (the child is the consumer; the parents are the recipients of the training), where the service supports IPP goals — e.g., parent training in behavior strategies, service-system navigation, or transition preparation? We understand code 108 (Parenting Support Services) serves consumers who are themselves parents, which is a different population.
2. For each code identified: what are the **current rates** (1:1 and group, if applicable), and is the rate set by the DDS rate model, median rate, or negotiation?
3. What **program design, staff qualifications, insurance, and background-check standards** would RCEB apply to vendorization under those codes — and under code 099 (General Self-Directed Supports), for which we intend to apply in parallel?
4. Can the identified services be **delivered remotely** (video) under current policy, or only in person? If remote delivery is not currently authorized, will these codes be considered in the remote-services eligible list DDS must issue by July 1, 2027 under this year's trailer bill?
5. For planning purposes: RCEB's **current typical timeline** from a complete vendorization application to a vendor number, and from a family's SDP orientation to budget certification.
6. **Application route for code 099:** DDS's vendorization page states that entities seeking to serve Self-Determination Program participants cannot apply in the Provider Directory (except FMS codes 315–317). Please confirm that a 099 (General Self-Directed Supports) application should be submitted as a traditional vendorization packet directly to RCEB, which forms RCEB currently requires, and whether families may separately access our pre-enrollment supports through Participant-Directed Services (099 sub-code PDS) while vendorization is in review.

We're happy to meet, and to share our program design in draft. Thank you — a written reply, even brief, helps us build to your standards from the start.

[Founder name] · Waypoint · [email/phone]
